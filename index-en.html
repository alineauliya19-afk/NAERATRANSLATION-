06 / Contract of Agreement

Contract of Agreement for Translation Services

Types of Documents for Consultation

Academic Translation

Essays, abstracts, academic articles, and learning materials.

General Documents

Personal letters, announcements, profiles, and general documents.

Articles & Digital Content

Articles, product descriptions, and social media content.

Sworn translations and official legalization are not included in standard services.

Service Agreement Terms

01

Scope of work

Clients provide documents, language direction, intended use, and special instructions.

02

Fees and payment

Prices and payment methods are agreed before work begins.

03

Turnaround time

Regular 7–8 days; Fasttrack 4–5 days after order confirmation.

04

Revisions and corrections

Corrections within the original instructions may be requested; scope changes are discussed separately.

05

Document confidentiality

Client documents are not published without permission.

06

Order confirmation

Work starts after the scope, price, and deadline are agreed.

On-time delivery commitment

If NAERA TRANSLATION misses the agreed deadline due to its own fault, one complimentary translation service may be provided subject to the terms, page limit, and conditions agreed in writing before work begins.

Specific terms such as revision limits, compensation limits, payment method, and final deadline must be confirmed in writing before work begins.
