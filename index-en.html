01 / ABOUT NAERA

Bridging languages, bringing people closer.

NAERA TRANSLATION is a translation service based in Bangkalan, East Java, offering English-to-Indonesian and Indonesian-to-English translation. We help students, individuals, and the wider community overcome language barriers through clear, accurate, easy-to-understand translations.

We prioritize the meaning of the original text, grammar, context, responsive communication, and document confidentiality. Each project and deadline is confirmed in advance to fit the client’s needs.

06 / SERVICE TERMS

Clear collaboration.
A smooth process.

Example preliminary service terms for NAERA TRANSLATION. Final details are agreed upon before work begins.

01

Scope of work

Clients provide the document, language pair, intended use, and any special instructions before work begins.

02

Fees and payment

Fees and payment methods are confirmed in advance. Work begins once both parties agree on scope, price, and schedule.

03

Turnaround time

Regular service is estimated at 7–8 days and Fasttrack at 4–5 days. Timing starts once the document and project details are complete and agreed.

04

Revisions and corrections

Clients may request corrections against the original instructions. Changes outside the initial scope may require additional time or fees agreed by both parties.

05

Document confidentiality

Client documents and information are handled carefully and will not be published as portfolio material without permission.

06

Agreement

An order is considered agreed upon once the client and NAERA TRANSLATION confirm the scope, fees, schedule, and terms in written communication.

These service terms provide a clear overview of the service process. Any order-specific scope, deadline, and fee will be confirmed in writing before work begins.
