06 / CONTRACT OF AGREEMENT

Contract of Agreement

The following is a summary of service terms. Final order details and approval must be confirmed by both parties before work begins.

Document Categories Available for Consultation

Academic documents, articles, presentation materials, general documents, and other text-based requests may be submitted for initial review. Acceptance depends on the content, complexity, and the team's capacity.

01. Scope of Work

The target language, page count, document format, and any special requirements are discussed before the order is confirmed.

02. Pricing and Payment

The Regular rate is Rp30,000 per page, and the Fasttrack rate is Rp65,000 per page. The total price and payment procedure are confirmed with the customer before work begins.

03. Turnaround Time

The estimated turnaround is 7–8 days for Regular and 4–5 days for Fasttrack. The agreed delivery schedule is communicated after the document has been reviewed.

04. Revisions

Revision requests may be discussed with the team. The revision scope and deadline should be agreed upon before placing an order.

05. Document Confidentiality

Customer documents are used for the agreed service. Permission from the document owner is required before translation results are used as portfolio samples.

06. Delays and Issue Resolution

If an issue affects the schedule, the team and customer will communicate to determine a solution based on their agreement. Any compensation terms must be agreed upon in writing before work begins.

07. Agreement and Approval

An order proceeds once the customer and team agree on the scope, price, schedule, and applicable terms.

Note: This section is a draft service information summary, not a finalized contract approved by both parties.
