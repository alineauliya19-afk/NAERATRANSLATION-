06 / CONTRACT OF AGREEMENT

Contract of Agreement

The following terms should be reviewed before confirming a translation order.

Document Categories Available for Consultation

Academic documents, articles, presentation materials, general documents, and other text-based requests may be submitted for consultation. Acceptance depends on the type, content, and complexity of the document.

01. Scope of Work

Customers provide the document, target language, page count, format, and any special requirements. The scope of work is agreed upon before translation begins.

02. Pricing and Payment

The Regular rate is Rp30,000 per page, while the Fasttrack rate is Rp65,000 per page. The total amount and payment method are confirmed before work begins.

03. Delivery Deadline

The estimated turnaround is 7–8 days for Regular and 4–5 days for Fasttrack. The binding deadline is the delivery date agreed upon when the order is confirmed.

04. Revisions

Customers may request revisions from the team. The revision scope and submission deadline are confirmed when placing an order.

05. Confidentiality

Customer documents are used for the agreed translation service. Publishing translation samples requires permission from the document owner.

06. On-Time Delivery Guarantee

If the NAERA TRANSLATION team fails to complete the work by the agreed deadline, the customer is entitled to one additional translation service free of charge.

The applicable page limit, document categories, and validity period of the free service must be agreed upon in writing before the order is confirmed.

07. Agreement and Approval

An order proceeds after the customer and the team agree on the scope of work, pricing, deadline, and service terms.

This is a draft summary of service terms. Final terms apply once approved by the customer and NAERA TRANSLATION.
